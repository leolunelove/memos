Audio conversion uses @ffmpeg/ffmpeg 0.12.15 (MIT) and @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10 (GPL-2.0-or-later).
Project and corresponding core build source: https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm/tree/main
The core WebAssembly binary is split into five byte-for-byte parts for delivery and reassembled unmodified before use.
